By neighborhood

A volume is counted in each of the (up to $areas_per_volume) community areas its footprint covers, so these counts sum to more than $n_volumes. Attribution is at the volume footprint, never the individual sheet: a sheet that fails to place has no coordinates, so a per-sheet map would drop exactly the failures it exists to show and report every neighborhood at 100% success.

$n_unlocated volumes ($pct_unlocated%) have no known footprint at all — no volunteer pins, no published layer, no bbox in the city config, and the LOC catalogue records no coordinates. A volume's neighborhood is not known until someone places it, so this view describes the placed corpus plus the fraction of the open work that a human has already located; the rest sits in "$unlocated_label".

This is not an accuracy report. Accuracy means residuals against human GCPs, and a volume can only be scored where human pins and scanned pixels meet — across this corpus that is $scoreable_phrase, because the image census fetched scans for precisely the volumes nobody had pinned. A per-neighborhood "median error" built on that would be a number from a handful of volumes wearing the clothes of a number from $n_volumes. Per-volume accuracy lives in autogeoref report, beside the counts of what it does and does not cover.
